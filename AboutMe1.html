Biodiversity:

Genetic Diversity: The variation in the genetic makeup within a species. This includes the diversity of genes within populations and individuals.
 Species Diversity: The variety of species within a given ecosystem or across the planet. It includes both the number of species (species richness) and their relative abundance. Ecosystem Diversity: The variety of ecosystems in a given area or on Earth as a whole. An ecosystem includes all living organisms in a particular area along with the physical environment (water, soil, air).
