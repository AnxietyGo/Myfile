Global Climate Change
*Definition: Refers to changes in the Earth's climate system on a global scale, influenced primarily by human activities and natural processes. Key Features: *Rising global temperatures due to the increase in greenhouse gas emissions. *Warming of both land and oceans. *Melting ice caps, glaciers, and rising sea levels. *Shifting weather patterns and increased frequency of extreme weather events (e.g., heatwaves, storms, floods).
