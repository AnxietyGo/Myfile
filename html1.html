The Nano World

Types and it's Definition:

The "nano world" refers to the realm of the extremely small, specifically on the scale of nanometers (1 nanometer = one billionth of a meter). At this scale, typically between 1 and 100 nanometers, the behavior of materials and physical properties often differ dramatically from what we experience in the macroscopic world.

Nanometers and Scale

Size: A nanometer (nm) is 1/1,000,000,000 of a meter. To put it in perspective, a human hair is about 80,000 to 100,000 nanometers thick, and a DNA molecule is about 2.5 nm in diameter. The nano world includes structures such as molecules, atoms, and nanoparticles, which are far too small to be seen with the naked eye, and even with a regular optical microscope.

Nanotechnology

Nanotechnology is the manipulation of matter on the atomic or molecular scale, typically at the level of 1–100 nm. It involves creating, modifying, or using nanomaterials for specific applications.

Nanoparticles

Nanoparticles are tiny particles with at least one dimension in the range of 1 to 100 nanometers (nm). These particles can be composed of various materials, such as metals, polymers, lipids, or ceramics, and they exhibit unique physical and chemical properties due to their small size and high surface area.
